Privacy Policy

How the Prism app for iPhone handles your information

Effective date: November 18, 2026

At a glance

  • Your tasks and other data are stored only on your iPhone.
  • There is no developer server. Your data is never sent to the developer or to third parties.
  • No advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Apple. The developer never receives your payment information.

This policy explains how NISHIMURA FUTURE WORKS (the “developer”), the developer of Prism, handles information in the iOS app “Prism” and its widgets (together, the “App”).

1. Information the App handles and where it is stored

The App stores the following information on your iPhone.

InformationDetails
TasksTitle, notes, due date, priority, reminder time, and when a task was completed or deleted
Categories and tagsName, color, icon, and order
SettingsDefault reminder, lock screen display, sound and haptics on/off, and similar options
Prism Pro statusWhether Prism Pro is unlocked or a purchase is waiting for approval (the purchase itself is managed by Apple)
  • Where it is stored: in a shared storage area on your iPhone (an App Group) that only the App and its widgets can use. Other apps cannot read it.
  • iCloud sync: the current version does not sync with iCloud or with your other devices.
  • iPhone backups: if you back up your iPhone (iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer), the App’s data may be included in that backup. Backups are an Apple feature; the developer cannot see their contents.
  • Deleting the App also deletes its data from your iPhone.

2. No data sharing, advertising, analytics, or tracking

  • The App does not send any of the information above, including the contents of your tasks, to the developer or to any third party. The developer does not operate a server.
  • The App contains no advertising, no analytics tools, and no tracking technology, including third-party SDKs.
  • The App does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and it does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • The App connects to the internet only to communicate with Apple’s App Store for purchases and restores, and when you open web pages such as this policy.
  • The App’s diagnostic log stays on your iPhone and never records task titles or notes.

3. Notifications

  • Reminders are local notifications that are scheduled and shown on your iPhone. No information is sent to a server for notifications.
  • The App asks for permission to send notifications the first time you set a reminder, after explaining why — not when you first open the App. If you decline, everything else in the App keeps working.
  • Notifications show the task title and due date. If you turn on Settings › Display › Show Only Counts When Locked in the App, notifications show only “Reminder” instead of the task title.
  • You can change notification permission at any time in iPhone Settings › Notifications › Prism.

4. Widgets

  • The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets (a Prism Pro feature) read today’s tasks from the same storage area on your iPhone. Nothing is sent off your device for widgets.
  • Widgets may show task titles, including on the Lock Screen. With Show Only Counts When Locked turned on, the Lock Screen widgets show only the number of tasks.

5. Device tilt (motion sensor)

While the App is on screen, it reads how your device is tilted to animate the light effects. These readings are used only for the animation and are never stored or sent. The App does not use “Motion & Fitness” data such as step counts. Tilt is not read when Reduce Motion or Low Power Mode is on.

6. In-app purchase (Prism Pro)

  • Buying Prism Pro (a one-time purchase), restoring it, and refunds are handled through Apple’s App Store (StoreKit).
  • Apple processes the payment. The developer does not receive your credit card or other payment information, or your Apple Account details.
  • The App checks on your iPhone whether Prism Pro is unlocked, using the purchase record that Apple provides to your device. The result (unlocked or not) is saved on your iPhone and shared with the widgets.
  • For how Apple handles your information, see Apple’s Privacy Policy.

7. Crash information

If you have turned on “Share With App Developers” in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple may provide the developer with information about App crashes (such as crash reports). Apple collects this information and provides it in a form that does not identify you. The developer uses it only to fix bugs. You can change this setting at any time.

8. Deleting your data

  • You can delete tasks one at a time. Deleted tasks move to Recently Deleted and are permanently deleted after 30 days. You can also delete them permanently sooner.
  • Completed tasks move to Recently Deleted automatically 30 days after they were completed.
  • Settings › Delete All Data in the App deletes all tasks, categories, tags, Recently Deleted items, and settings, and cancels scheduled notifications. This can’t be undone. Your Prism Pro purchase is recorded in your Apple Account, so it is not removed.
  • Deleting the App from your iPhone also deletes its data.
  • The developer does not hold any of your task data, so there is nothing for the developer to delete on its side.

9. Information you send when contacting us

We accept inquiries through a web form (Google Forms). The email address and message you enter in the form are handled as follows.

  • They are used only to reply to you and to investigate and fix problems.
  • They are not shared with third parties, except where required by law.
  • Form responses are stored on Google’s services. For how Google handles information, see Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • They are kept only as long as needed to handle your inquiry and then deleted. You can also ask us to delete them.

The form is a web page outside the App. The App cannot read what you enter in it.

10. Children

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. If a child wants to contact us, please do so together with a parent or guardian. We will promptly delete an inquiry at a parent’s or guardian’s request.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when we add features (for example, iCloud sync) or when laws change. When we do, we will update this page and the date at the top. If the information the App handles changes significantly, we will also let you know, for example in the App’s release notes.

This policy is also available in Japanese. If there is any difference between the two, the Japanese version prevails.
