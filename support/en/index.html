Getting started

Which devices and iOS versions are supported?

Prism is an iPhone app and requires iOS 26 or later.

The App is available in English and Japanese and follows your iPhone’s language. To use a different language for Prism only, go to iPhone Settings › Apps › Prism › Language.

Where is my data stored? Can I sync with iCloud?

Your tasks and other data are stored only on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to the developer’s servers.

The current version does not sync with iCloud or your other devices. Deleting the App also deletes your data.

For details, see the Privacy Policy.

Notifications

I’m not getting reminder notifications

Please check the following, in order.

  1. Notification permission: if the App’s Settings › Notifications shows “Off — Open Settings,” notifications aren’t allowed. Tap it to open iPhone Settings and turn on Allow Notifications. You can also change this in iPhone Settings › Notifications › Prism.
  2. Focus: when a Focus such as Do Not Disturb is on, notifications may not appear. Check whether Prism is allowed in iPhone Settings › Focus.
  3. Reminder time: reminders set for a time that has already passed aren’t delivered. Open the task and make sure the reminder is in the future.
  4. Completed or deleted tasks: tasks that are completed or in Recently Deleted don’t send reminders.
  5. Due dates without a time: reminders for these tasks arrive at the time set in the App’s Settings › Notifications › All-Day Reminder Time.
  6. A large number of reminders: iPhone limits how many notifications one app can schedule, so Prism schedules the nearest ones first. Opening the App from time to time, or keeping iPhone Settings › General › Background App Refresh on, lets later reminders be scheduled as their turn comes.

After you allow notifications, open the App and the reminders you already set will be scheduled.

Notifications say only “Reminder” instead of the task name

Settings › Display › Show Only Counts When Locked is turned on in the App. While it’s on, notifications never show task names, even when your iPhone is unlocked (and Lock Screen widgets show only the count).

If you only want to hide notification content while your iPhone is locked, you can turn this setting off and set iPhone Settings › Notifications › Show Previews to “When Unlocked.”

Tapping “Complete” on a notification asks me to unlock my iPhone

To prevent someone else from completing your tasks, completing a task from a notification always requires unlocking your iPhone.

Prism Pro and widgets

What is Prism Pro? What’s included for free?

Prism Pro is a one-time purchase that you keep forever (not a subscription). The price is shown in the App’s purchase screen.

Up to 5 categories (including the built-in “Work” and “Personal”) and up to 10 tags Unlimited categories and tags

You can buy it from Settings › Prism Pro in the App. Prism Pro does not support Family Sharing.

I can’t create a new category or tag

The free version lets you create up to 5 categories (including the built-in “Work” and “Personal”) and up to 10 tags. Settings › Manage Categories & Tags shows how many you’re using, such as “4 / 5.”

  • Deleting a category or tag you no longer use frees up a slot. When you delete a category, its tasks aren’t deleted; they become “No Category.”
  • Adding an existing tag to a task doesn’t count toward the limit.
  • You can always rename, recolor, change icons, and reorder.
  • With Prism Pro, you can create as many as you like.
How do I add a widget? (Prism Pro)

Widgets are a Prism Pro feature.

Home Screen

  1. Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen, tap “Edit” in the top-left corner, then tap “Add Widget.”
  2. Choose “Prism” from the list.
  3. Pick a size of “Today’s Tasks” (small, medium, or large) and add it. The medium and large sizes let you complete tasks right from the widget.

Lock Screen

  1. Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap “Customize,” then choose “Lock Screen.”
  2. Tap the widget area and add “Tasks Left Today” from Prism.

If you haven’t bought Prism Pro, the widget shows “Available with Prism Pro,” and tapping it opens the purchase screen.

If a widget says “Open Prism to continue” or looks out of date, open the App once.

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap “Restore Purchases” below Settings › Prism Pro in the App. You can also tap “Restore Purchases” in the purchase screen.

  • You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Prism Pro. If you see “No purchase to restore,” check your App Store account.
  • With the same Apple Account, Prism Pro usually comes back automatically when you reinstall the App or open it on a new iPhone.
  • If you see “Waiting for approval,” the purchase is waiting for a family organizer or parent to approve it (Ask to Buy). Once it’s approved, Prism Pro unlocks automatically.

Delete All Data does not remove your Prism Pro purchase.

I’d like a refund

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple; the developer can’t process them. Please request one at Apple’s Report a Problem page.

After a refund, Prism Pro features are no longer available, but your categories, tags, and tasks stay as they are (even ones over the free limit; you just can’t create new ones).

Storing and deleting tasks

What happens to completed and deleted tasks? (the 30-day rule)
  • Completed tasks stay in Completed and move to Recently Deleted automatically 30 days after you complete them.
  • Deleted tasks move to Recently Deleted and are permanently deleted 30 days after they arrive there. Each task shows how many days are left.
  • Right after you delete a task, you can tap “Undo” at the bottom of the screen.
  • While a task is in Recently Deleted, you can “Restore” it at any time (tasks you choose to Delete Permanently can’t be restored). Its category, tags, due date, and reminder come back too (except categories or tags you deleted in the meantime). A completed task you restore starts a new 30-day period from the day you restore it.

Open Completed and Recently Deleted from the “…” button at the top right of the All screen. Recently Deleted is also in Settings › Data.

How do I delete my data?
  • One task: swipe left on a task in a list, or touch and hold it and choose “Delete.” You can also delete it from the task’s details. It moves to Recently Deleted.
  • All completed tasks: tap “Delete All” in Completed to move them to Recently Deleted.
  • Permanently, right away: in Recently Deleted, choose “Delete Permanently” for a task, or “Delete All.”
  • Everything: in the App, go to Settings › Delete All Data. You’ll be asked to confirm twice; the second time, hold “Hold to Delete” for 1.5 seconds or type “Delete.” All tasks, categories, tags, Recently Deleted items, and settings are deleted, only the “Work” and “Personal” categories remain, and scheduled notifications are canceled. This can’t be undone. Your Prism Pro purchase is not removed.

Deleting the App from your iPhone also deletes its data. The developer doesn’t hold any of your data, so there’s nothing to delete on the developer’s side.

Can I move my data to a new iPhone?

Prism has no feature to sync or transfer data through iCloud or other services. Your data is stored only on your iPhone.

  • If you set up your new iPhone by restoring from an iCloud or computer backup or by using Quick Start, your Prism data may come along. Depending on the method and situation, it may not, and the developer can’t guarantee the transfer.
  • After moving, you may be asked to allow notifications again.
  • Prism Pro works on your new iPhone with the same Apple Account (see How do I restore my purchase?).

Before you give away your old iPhone, make sure your data appears on the new one.

Other questions

I don’t hear the completion sound

Sounds don’t play while your iPhone is in Silent mode. Also make sure Settings › Haptics & Sound › Completion Sound is on in the App. Completing a task from a widget or notification doesn’t play a sound.

I see “Couldn’t open your data”

Tap “Try Again,” or restart your iPhone and open the App again. If that doesn’t help, please contact us below without deleting the App (deleting the App also deletes your data).
